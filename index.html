January checklist

  • Pack thermals, a down jacket, gloves, a woolen cap and waterproof boots with grip.
  • Ride in daylight only. Sunset is near 5:30 pm and shaded bends get black ice.
  • Carry a driving licence and ID for the bike rentals, and confirm rental and deposit rules ahead.
  • Book both trains early and keep a 3-4 hour buffer on Day 10, since fog delays northern trains.
  • Ask every hotel about heating and hot water. Confirm Kainchi Dham hours a day ahead.

Where to find snow in January

  • Likely: Dhanaulti, plus Auli, Chopta and Munsiyari if you add them.
  • Possible: Mussoorie, Nainital, Binsar and Kausani.
  • Rare: Almora, Kainchi Dham, Jageshwar and Ranikhet. Cold, but snow is occasional.
  • None: Haridwar, Rishikesh, Dehradun and Corbett.
  • This is a rough guide. Snowfall changes every year, so check the forecast a few days before you go.
